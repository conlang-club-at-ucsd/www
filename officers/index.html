Meet the officers

These are your lovely officers for this year. Come say hi!

Sina

President

Any/All

I'm Sina, president of Conlang Club. I've been a conlanger for roughly 10 years now, working on a conlang now called Ravya. I started Ravya as a language intended to be spoken by llamas in a science fiction book I wanted to write when I was in 5th/6th grade; through that I developed a love for linguistics and have been making that conlang ever since!

Ravya is one of my favorite things to talk about, but I'm also huge nerd about a buncha other stuff like world history, culinary history, food science, vexillology, photography, zoology, physiology, and almost everything else under the sun. Please reach out to me whenever, for conlanging reasons or otherwise :D

Alex

Officer

She/It

Hi! I'm the main developer for conlang club-related things, like this website. I'm a prolific conlanger, with over 30 full (ish) conlangs under my belt. My favorite is Aurathil (which by the way, you can see on the member conlangs page), and its Wind-Sensed Evidential.

I'm also a huge fanatic for worldbuilding, and have been working on my own world for a couple years now! I'm usually making Minecraft mods or writing music, but occasionally you can see me doing low-level things in c. I've also made a couple of programming languages, including one that I call Invisible Ink, because all of the characters you write are either zero-width or whitespace utf-encoded characters.

Bridget

Officer

Any except He/It

Salutations! I'm Bridget, I exist, and I sort of dabble in everything with regard to the club. I'm a Principal Member, which probably means officer; I do admin things for our Discord; I frequently book locations for our club meetings; and I am technically also involved with the website, though I have much less experience than Alex. I am a writer and worldbuilder and possess a separate website which is currently undergoing a redesign.

I started conlanging in middle school, had to stop during high school for overwhelming-workload reasons, and courtesy of college have finally had enough free time to start it up again. My current project, sort of a softball before I have to embark on a bunch of a posteriori langs for the Imperium Novel, is an a priori Cthulhu-Mythos trade language called Intercultist, which is what you get when a bunch of cultists and sorcerers reverse-engineer their chants and their traditional meanings (which are most commonly lossy vocal imitations of phrases in various partially-psychic languages and missing important pieces of the original grammar) and decide to use that to communicate cross-denominationally.

(I would like to give a particular shout-out to Fīrhen, my most developed middle-school lang, which had what I can only describe as emotion-driven allophony affecting every sound in the language; it probably would have made its speakers’ understanding of phonemes absolutely fascinating. I've considered un-sunsetting the worldbuilding project it was for just for its sake.)
